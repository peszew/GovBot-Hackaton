# GovBot -- AI Assistant for Local Governments in mObywatel

**1st Place** -- Ministry of Digital Affairs Challenge at the **Econverse Hackathon** (March 2025)

## About

GovBot is an AI-powered conversational assistant designed to be integrated into mObywatel -- the official Polish government mobile app with over 10 million active users. It provides citizens with instant, 24/7 access to local government information through both text and voice interactions.

Built in 24 hours during the Econverse Hackathon (Smart Cities track).
